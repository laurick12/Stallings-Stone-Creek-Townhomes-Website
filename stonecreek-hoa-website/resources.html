HOA vs. Homeowner Maintenance Matrix

Quick reference so you know whether to submit a RealManage / portal request or call a private vendor.

Component / Issue Responsible Party Notes & Next Step
Roof Shingles & Gutters HOA (Townhome Services) Report roof leaks, flashing, or gutter/downspout issues via RealManage / portal; structural trusses/joists are excluded from Exterior Maintenance.
Siding, Trim & Exterior Brick HOA (Townhome Services) Original exterior finish, trim, shutters, and painting covered under Townhome Services; owner exterior modifications are excluded from HOA maintenance.
Party Walls (Shared Walls) Homeowner / Co-Owners Owners maintain interior finishes (paint, drywall/plaster) and attachments on their side; adjoining Party Wall Co-Owners share joint responsibility for structural elements.
Exterior Lights, Decks, Stairs & Porches Homeowner (Porch Roof/Columns: HOA) Exterior light fixtures/bulbs, patios, terraces, decks, and stairs are owner responsibility; HOA Exterior Maintenance covers front porch roof and decorative columns.
Windows, Screens & Glass Seals Homeowner Windows, screens, and locks are owner responsibility; contact management/insurance if casualty loss involves covered Building Shell components.
Garage Door, Springs & Opener Homeowner Opener, springs, and weather-stripping maintained by owner; repairs/alterations require ARB approval where applicable.
HVAC, Heat Pump & Water Heater Homeowner HVAC equipment and mechanical/plumbing systems are owner responsibility; see vetted HVAC/Plumbing pros in the vendor directory.
Interior Plumbing & Main Shutoff Homeowner Unit pipes and fixtures are owner responsibility, except HOA maintains water supply line to inside main shutoff valve (or to curb stop if meter is inside).
Termite, Pest & Vapor Barrier HOA (Townhome Services / Exterminating) HOA provides termite/wood-infestation treatment and bonds for Townhome Buildings plus annual vapor barrier/mitigation inspections and recommended repairs.
Front Lawn, Yard & Common Mulch Beds HOA (Landscaping / Common) Mowing, bed care, and approved landscaping maintained by HOA/BrightView; excludes fenced/enclosed areas, patios/courtyards, and unapproved owner plantings.
Rear Patio Slab & Driveway Pad HOA (Driveway) / Homeowner (Patio) HOA covers driveway Exterior Maintenance; homeowner maintains rear patio slab (keep clean, no items on railing edges) and gets ARB approval before alterations.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common homeowner questions.

What is the role of the HOA Board of Directors?
The Board of Directors is made up of volunteers — your neighbors — who dedicate personal time to overseeing community affairs alongside full-time jobs, families, and busy schedules (noting that during the Declarant Control Period, the Declarant appoints the Board and holds special voting rights under the CC&Rs). Their responsibilities include adopting budgets, enforcing CC&Rs and rules, overseeing architectural reviews, managing vendors, and protecting community standards. Please be patient and respectful — volunteers serve with limited time.
What do my HOA fees cover?
Your monthly assessment ($155.00/month for standard homeowners in FY 2026) funds Association operations and reserves across Common Expenses and Townhome Expenses. Depending on applicability under the CC&Rs, this covers Common Area and landscaping maintenance, stormwater and street lighting expenses, Townhome exterior/building-shell insurance and designated Townhome Services, and contributions toward the replacement/reserve fund.
How do I submit an Architectural Review (ARC) request?
Exterior changes, additions, or modifications — including paint/exterior finishes, fencing, walls, play/game structures, satellite dishes/antennas, and qualifying landscaping changes — require prior written approval from the Architectural Review Board (ARB). Use the ARC Pre-Check tool above if available, then submit your request through the Resident Portal with plans, photos, colors/materials, and details. Under the CC&Rs, the ARB has 45 days after receiving required plans and specifications to approve, conditionally approve, or reject a submission.
How do I reserve the playground or pavilion?
Use the Playground & Pavilion Reservations section. Pick your preferred date and time block, check availability, and submit your request for confirmation. Any use of Common Areas or play structures must also follow the CC&Rs and Board-adopted policies (such as the 6-hour limit and no-vehicle-on-grass rule for bounce houses).
Who do I contact for community concerns or rule violations?
For rule violations, common-area damage, noise complaints, or parking issues, contact management at screekth@ciramail.com . For general feedback, use the Board feedback form. For urgent safety emergencies, call 911 or emergency services first. While interior unit maintenance is generally the homeowner's responsibility, the Association handles designated Common Area and Townhome Services (including exterior water supply up to the interior main shutoff valve or curb stop, and annual vapor barrier inspections/recommended repairs).
How can I help the community?
✓ Pay your HOA dues on time. Late payments strain the budget and can trigger fees and collection costs.

✓ Respond promptly to violation notices. Resolving issues quickly avoids follow-ups, administrative/enforcement costs, and potential legal action.

✓ Care for your unit and follow property rules. Keep your home and patio clean, store trash bins properly, follow ARB rules before making exterior changes, and avoid placing items on balcony/terrace railing edges.

✓ Follow parking rules. Park only in designated parking spaces, keep alleys clear, and never park on streets or at alleyway ends.

✓ Attend community meetings. Stay informed and participate when meetings are held.

✓ Report issues early. Let management know right away about maintenance concerns or damage so small issues don't escalate.

✓ Be kind to volunteers and staff. Constructive feedback is welcome; hostility is not.
How do I get involved or join the Board?
Board elections and governance follow the Governing Documents and North Carolina law (with the Declarant appointing directors during the Declarant Control Period). Eligible homeowners can share their interest through the Board feedback form — community involvement is always welcome.
What happens if I don’t pay my HOA dues?
Assessments unpaid for more than 30 days may incur a late fee (the greater of 10% or $20), interest (up to 18% per annum or the maximum allowed by law), acceleration of remaining fiscal-year assessments, and collection/attorney fees. Continued delinquency can lead to a claim of lien, foreclosure or legal action, rent collection if leased, and — after proper notice and hearing — suspension of voting rights, non-essential services, and non-access/non-drainage Common Area privileges. If you face hardship, reach out to management early.
Where can I find the Master Insurance Policy, CC&Rs, bylaws, and meeting minutes?
Governing documents, policies, financial/budget materials, and notices are available in the Resident Portal (or through management). If you need a lender Certificate of Insurance (COI) or estoppel/payment certificate, check the portal instructions or contact management.
