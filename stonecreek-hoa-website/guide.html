Community Parking & No-Tow Map

Trout Lily Dr, Autumn Olive Dr & Alley rules
Toggle
  • No commercial vehicles, trailers, boats, or RVs at any time unless stored inside an enclosed garage (temporary deliveries/service and resident law enforcement vehicles exempt)
  • Trout Lily Drive: No parking permitted at any time (vehicles must use designated parking spaces)
  • Autumn Olive Drive: Parking allowed only in designated parking spaces on the right side (no street parking outside designated spaces)
  • Alleys: No parking, or storage permitted in any alley or at alleyway ends without exception (12 mph speed limit)
  • Towing & Enforcement: N Style Towing / Lifted Towing (704-201-2704) conducts random patrols 7:00 PM – 7:00 AM (or tows upon HOA request); violators are towed at the owner's expense ($300 impound + $35/day storage)

Violators subject to towing at owner's expense. See full CC&Rs in the Resident Portal.
