Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common homeowner questions.

What is the role of the HOA Board of Directors?
The Board of Directors is made up entirely of unpaid volunteers — your neighbors — who dedicate their personal time to manage community affairs on top of full-time jobs, families, and busy lives. Their responsibilities include overseeing the community budget, enforcing CC&Rs, approving architectural requests, coordinating with vendors, and making decisions that protect property values. Please be patient and respectful — they are serving voluntarily with limited time.
What do my HOA fees cover?
Your monthly assessment covers common area maintenance and landscaping, exterior building insurance (master policy), playground and pavilion upkeep, street and parking lot maintenance, community lighting, and contributions to our reserve fund for future capital repairs.
How do I submit an Architectural Review (ARC) request?
All exterior modifications — including paint colors, fencing, patio additions, satellite dishes, and landscaping changes — require prior approval. Log in to the Resident Portal and submit your ARC request with a description, photos, and relevant materials. The Board typically reviews within 30 days.
How do I reserve the playground or pavilion?
Open the Playground & Pavilion card in the Neighborhood Guide section above. You can view the live availability calendar and select “Request a reservation” to submit a booking form. Reservations are in 4-hour blocks. You’ll receive an automated confirmation email if the slot is available.
Who do I contact for community concerns or rule violations?
For rule violations, common-area damage, noise complaints, or parking issues, contact our management company at screekth@ciramail.com. For general feedback, use the Board feedback form. Please note: the HOA is not responsible for maintenance inside your individual unit — for in-home emergencies, contact a vendor from our Recommended list directly.
How can I help the community?
✓ Pay your HOA dues on time. Late payments strain the budget and delay projects.

✓ Respond promptly to violation notices. Each unresolved violation costs the HOA time and money in follow-ups, management fees, and potential legal action — expenses that come out of everyone’s assessments.

✓ Maintain your property. Keep your lawn trimmed, trash bins stored, and exterior clean.

✓ Follow parking rules. Use your garage and driveway first.

✓ Attend community meetings. Your voice matters.

✓ Report issues early. Let the management company know before small problems become big ones.

✓ Be kind to volunteers. Constructive feedback is welcome; hostility is not.
How do I get involved or join the Board?
Board elections are held at the Annual Meeting each year. Any homeowner in good standing is eligible to run. Submit your interest through the Board feedback form — we welcome community involvement!
What happens if I don’t pay my HOA dues?
Past-due accounts are subject to late fees. Continued delinquency may result in collections and ultimately a lien on your property. If you’re experiencing hardship, contact the management company early to discuss arrangements.
Where can I find the CC&Rs, bylaws, and meeting minutes?
All governing documents are available in the Resident Portal. Log in and navigate to the Documents section.
